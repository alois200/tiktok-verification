Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Pet Safety Social Media respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the service.

Information We Process

The service may process information required to connect authorized social media accounts and to create and publish social media content.

TikTok Data

When a user connects a TikTok account, the service may process information provided through the official TikTok API, including account authorization information necessary to provide the requested functionality.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide and operate the service, including creating, managing, and publishing authorized social media content.

Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may only be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the service or when required by law.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service. Users may request deletion of their information and revoke TikTok authorization at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, please contact the service operator.
